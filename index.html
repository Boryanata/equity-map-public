About these data

The public computer center points and reported services come from NYC Open Data’s 2024 Citywide Public Computer Centers file. The 2020 NTA boundaries come from the NYC Department of City Planning. The Connected Communities and Digital Investment District classifications come from project files supplied for this prototype. A center absent from the Connected Communities list is not necessarily unfunded. District categories are high and very high; no numeric need scores were supplied.
